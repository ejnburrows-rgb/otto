# OTTO Plumbing Data Retention Policy

This policy outlines how data collected by the OTTO Plumbing CRM (via the mobile and web application) is stored, used, and retained.

## 1. GPS Location Data
- **What is collected:** When a field worker clocks into a job or changes their status, their device captures the current GPS coordinates.
- **Why it is collected:** To verify timesheet accuracy and provide liability protection for arrival/departure times at job sites.
- **Retention Period:** GPS records are kept for **30 days** in the active database. After 30 days, they are archived and automatically deleted after **1 year** (unless required longer for an active legal or HR dispute).

## 2. Job Photos & Documents
- **What is collected:** Photos of job sites, plumbing issues, completed work, and signed estimates/invoices.
- **Why it is collected:** To document before/after conditions, protect against liability claims, and ensure quality standards.
- **Retention Period:** Job-related photos and documents are kept for **7 years** to comply with standard insurance and tax liability requirements.

## 3. Communication Logs (SMS/Email)
- **What is collected:** SMS and email messages sent to customers via the OTTO platform.
- **Why it is collected:** To maintain a complete audit trail of customer interactions and estimates.
- **Retention Period:** Customer communication logs are kept indefinitely as part of the permanent customer record, unless the customer requests deletion under applicable privacy laws.

## 4. Employee Data
- **What is collected:** Sign-in histories, time off requests, and internal messages.
- **Why it is collected:** For payroll processing and operational management.
- **Retention Period:** General employee logs are kept for **3 years** after the employee departs the company.
